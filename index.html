Star Picking

green circle = star in image associated with star in catalogue
red circle = star in catalogue not yet associated with star in image
S + click selects an image star; then click the matching red catalogue star. You might need to scan around a bit to find the star you are looking for.
Left drag moves the view, right drag rotates it, and the wheel scales the view.
X toggles between the star catalogue view and the image, which is useful while checking a match.
